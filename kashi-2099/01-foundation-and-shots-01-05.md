**Rules for every clip**
- Generate the still image first, then use it as the first frame for image-to-video. Never text-to-video for a shot with a character.
- Always attach the approved character reference image when generating a still that contains that character.
- No readable text inside generated frames. Countdown, labels and titles are added in the editor.
- Dialogue shots are framed so lips are small, turned, or off screen. Voice is recorded separately and laid over.
- Subtitles: Telugu only, font Noto Sans Telugu, white with soft black shadow, lower third, max 2 lines.

### SHOT 1 — Kashi 2099, aerial

1. **Shot:** 1
2. **Timeline:** 0:00–0:10
3. **Location:** Air above the Ganga, looking west toward the ghats, sunset
4. **Characters:** None
5. **Visual description:** The crescent of the west-bank ghats backlit by a low golden sun. The old city is untouched: stone steps, sandstone buildings, temple shikharas, a distant golden spire. On the opposite east bank and far behind the old city stand slim glass towers. Small flying vehicles move in orderly lanes high above the river, never above the temples. Wooden boats on the water.
6. **Image prompt:**
```
Massive aerial establishing shot of Varanasi in the year 2099 at golden sunset, camera high above the Ganga looking toward the crescent-shaped west bank, continuous stone ghat steps descending to the water, tightly packed historic sandstone buildings and temple shikharas left completely unaltered, a distant golden temple spire among the old city rooftops, slim futuristic glass towers standing only on the far east bank and far on the horizon behind the old city, small sleek flying vehicles in orderly lanes high above the river, wooden boats on the water, low sun behind the old city casting long golden light and haze across the river, photorealistic cinematic film still, shot on ARRI Alexa 65 with anamorphic lenses, fine film grain, teal and amber color grade, volumetric light, high dynamic range, 16:9 aspect ratio
```
7. **Image-to-video prompt:**
```
Slow smooth aerial push forward toward the ghats. Flying vehicles glide steadily along their lanes. The river water ripples gently and boats drift. Sunlight haze shifts slightly. Buildings and temples stay perfectly still and unchanged. Cinematic, steady, no camera shake.
```
8. **Lens and movement:** 24 mm anamorphic equivalent, drone-style forward push with a very slight descent
9. **Lighting and VFX:** Backlit golden hour, atmospheric haze, soft lens flare. VFX limited to vehicles and far towers
10. **Expression and action:** None
11. **Telugu audio:** Hook (0:00–0:03): "భూమికి మిగిలింది పది నిమిషాలు. ఆ విషయం తెలిసింది ఒక్కడికే." Narration (0:04–0:10): "సంవత్సరం 2099… వేల సంవత్సరాల చరిత్ర కలిగిన కాశీ… ఇప్పుడు భవిష్యత్తు సాంకేతికతకు కేంద్రంగా మారింది."
